Our love story

One unexpected message.
One answered prayer.

It all began in 2025 at a church activity in Sta. Rosa, Nueva Ecija, where a little teasing about being single led to an unexpected message—and the beginning of our love story.

What started as simple conversations soon grew into something beautiful. Through daily messages, shared dreams, and life’s challenges, we found love in each other. In April 2026, we officially began our journey as a couple, choosing to grow, forgive, and love each other through every season.

Now, by God’s grace, we are ready to begin our forever. Before Him, we will make a sacred promise to love, cherish, and remain faithful to one another for the rest of our lives.

From one unexpected message to a lifetime of love, our story has become one of God’s most beautiful answered prayers. And now, our forever begins. ♡
